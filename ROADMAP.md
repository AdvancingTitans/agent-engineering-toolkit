# Roadmap

## Now — v1.18 Activation and Distribution

- Installed stale-proof demo.
- Focused README, PyPI, Chinese docs, and static Pages assets.
- Community health, five Skill catalog, read-only Action template.
- Aggregate maintainer measurement without product telemetry.

Core capability is frozen for 14 days around the release candidate except for
demo, installation, compatibility, documentation, and security blockers.

## Next — after real use

- Publish reproducible stale-proof, scope-drift, and cross-Agent cases.
- Improve integrations from real host smoke tests.
- Support small, fixture-backed external contributions.

## Later — conditional

Homebrew/Scoop/Nix, broader IDE or CI integrations, registry publication,
attestations, conference material, and enterprise templates require evidence
of real usage and maintenance capacity. Dates are intentionally not promised.
