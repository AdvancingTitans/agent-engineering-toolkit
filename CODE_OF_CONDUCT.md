# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be precise and constructive. Critique claims, evidence, code, and documented
behavior—not people. Respect privacy, attribution, accessibility needs, and
different levels of experience. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox,
spam, manipulate engagement, or publish another person's confidential data.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior threatens safety, collaboration, or the
project's evidence standards. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately where appropriate, and free of retaliation.

Report conduct concerns through the private channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), without placing sensitive details in a public
Issue.
