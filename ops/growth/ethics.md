# Growth ethics

AET growth work must never buy, exchange, automate, or incentivize GitHub
Stars. It must not use fake accounts, coordinated voting, mass direct
messages, automated comments, or misleading case studies.

No script in this repository may post to GitHub, Hacker News, Reddit, V2EX,
Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn, Zhihu, Juejin, an Awesome List, or an external
registry. Hacker News submissions and comments require a human maintainer's
own writing and must not ask for votes.

Repository-level aggregate metrics may be read for maintainer decisions.
Stargazer identities, device identifiers, product usage, local AET commands,
and individual behavior must not be collected.

Every public claim must remain reproducible, state its observed scope, preserve
limitations, and avoid presenting Stars as proof of software quality.
